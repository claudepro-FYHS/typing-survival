# 打字求生 Typing Survival

**開始玩 / Play:** https://claudepro-fyhs.github.io/typing-survival/

用電腦的 Chrome 打開，需要實體鍵盤。  
Open it in Chrome on a computer with a real keyboard.

## 中文說明

你站在畫面中間，喪屍從四面八方走過來，每隻頭上都有一個英文單字。把字打出來就能擊倒牠。喪屍走到你身邊會一直咬你，血量歸零就結束。

### 怎麼玩
1. 在主畫面選**模式**、**難度**和**年段**（年段決定出現哪些單字）。
2. 按「開始」，直接打喪屍頭上的字，不用按 Enter 或空白鍵。
3. 撐越多波、打得越準，分數越高。

### 兩種模式
- **傳統模式**：打了第一個字母就鎖定那隻喪屍，必須把牠的字打完才能打別隻。
- **自由模式**：打到一半可以隨時改打別隻，已經打的進度會保留（虛線框）。之後接著打牠的下一個字母就能回來。  
  例：`apple` 打了 `ap`，改打 `b` 去射 `boy`，再打 `p` 就回到 `apple`。  
  注意：換目標時連擊會歸零；沒有任何喪屍需要你按的那個字母才算打錯。

### 喪屍
- **綠色**：普通。
- **黃色**：跑得快，字很短。
- **紫色**：很耐打，要連續打完兩個字。

### 道具
喪屍有時會掉下箱子，把箱子上的字打完就能拿到道具。用數字鍵使用，每種最多帶 3 個。

- `1` **炸彈**：清掉身邊的喪屍。
- `2` **冰凍**：所有喪屍停住 5 秒。
- `3` **醫療包**：回復 35 點生命。
- `4` **護盾**：6 秒內不會受傷。

### 分數
- 每打對一個字母得 10 分，連續打對會有連擊加成（每 10 連擊 +0.1 倍，最高 3 倍）。
- 擊倒喪屍和過關都有額外獎勵。打錯會中斷連擊。

### 其他按鍵
- `Esc`：暫停（多人連線時不會暫停，只會開啟選單）。
- 打不出字的話，請檢查是否開著中文輸入法或 Caps Lock。

### 多人連線（2–6 人合作）
1. 主畫面按「多人連線」，輸入名字。
2. 一個人按「建立房間」，把畫面上的 5 碼**房號**告訴同學。
3. 其他人輸入房號，按「加入房間」。
4. 人到齊後房主按「開始」。模式、難度和詞庫用房主在主畫面選的設定。

合作規則：
- **一起打同一隻**：每個人各打各的進度，全隊打對的字母加起來夠了，喪屍就倒下。大隻的一起打比較快。
- **倒下**：血量歸零會倒下，頭上出現紅色的字。**所有還站著的隊友都把那個字打完**，你才會復活。
- **全員倒下**就結束。
- 房主請不要關掉網頁或切換到別的分頁，否則整個房間會卡住或結束。
- 多人連線的成績不進排行榜。

### 金幣和商店
- 登入並填好資料後，每玩一場（單人或多人）都會拿到金幣。撐越多波、打對越多字母、難度越高，金幣越多。
- 每天前 3 場拿全額，之後每場只有三成。不到 30 秒的場次沒有金幣。
- 主畫面按「商店 · 角色」可以買**角色**、**造型**、**場景**和**武器**。
  - 角色開場會多帶一個道具或有小能力（例如醫護兵帶 1 個醫療包）。
  - 造型、場景、武器只改變外觀。買造型前要先擁有那個角色。
  - 有些造型不能買，要達成成就才會解鎖（例如累計救起隊友 100 次）。
- 多人連線時，隊友看得到你的角色和造型；場景用房主的。

### 登入和排行榜
- 用 Google 帳號登入後，單人模式的成績會上排行榜。不登入也可以玩。
- 學校帳號第一次登入要填班級和座號（例：`J203`、`S1S2`、`S1AC1`），填了就不能自己改，要改請找老師。
- 學校帳號可以看**我的班級**、**我的年段**、**全校**三種排行榜；其他 Google 帳號進**公開榜**。
- 每種模式和難度各有自己的排行榜，只記錄你今年的最佳成績。每年重新開始。

## English

You stand in the middle of the field. Zombies walk in from every side, each with an English word above its head. Type the word to knock it down. A zombie that reaches you keeps biting; the game ends when your health runs out.

### How to play
1. On the main screen choose a **mode**, a **difficulty** and a **grade** (the grade decides which words appear).
2. Press Start and type the words above the zombies. No Enter or space needed.
3. Survive more waves and type accurately for a higher score.

### Two modes
- **Classic (傳統模式):** the first letter locks you onto that zombie, and you must finish its word before typing another.
- **Free (自由模式):** switch to another zombie at any time. Your progress on the first one is kept (dashed frame), and typing its next letter takes you back.  
  Example: after `ap` of `apple`, type `b` to shoot `boy`, then `p` to return to `apple`.  
  Switching resets your combo. A key only counts as a mistake when no zombie needs that letter.

### Zombies
- **Green:** normal.
- **Yellow:** fast, with short words.
- **Purple:** tough, needs two words in a row.

### Items
Zombies sometimes drop a crate. Type the word on the crate to pick it up, then use it with a number key. You can carry up to 3 of each.

- `1` **Bomb:** clears the zombies near you.
- `2` **Freeze:** stops every zombie for 5 seconds.
- `3` **Medkit:** restores 35 health.
- `4` **Shield:** no damage for 6 seconds.

### Score
- 10 points per correct letter, multiplied by your combo (+0.1 for every 10 in a row, up to 3x).
- Extra points for each zombie knocked down and each wave cleared. A mistake breaks the combo.

### Other keys
- `Esc`: pause (in co-op the game keeps running and a menu opens instead).
- If nothing happens when you type, check for a Chinese input method or Caps Lock.

### Co-op (2–6 players)
1. Press 多人連線 on the main screen and enter a name.
2. One player presses 建立房間 (create room) and tells the others the 5-character **room code**.
3. The others enter the code and press 加入房間 (join room).
4. The host presses 開始 (start). The host's mode, difficulty and word list are used.

Co-op rules:
- **Shared targets:** everyone has their own progress on a word, and the zombie falls when the team's correct letters add up to its length. Big ones go down faster together.
- **Down:** at zero health you fall and a red word appears above you. You get back up once **every teammate still standing has typed that word**.
- The game ends when **everyone is down**.
- The host should keep the game tab open and in front; closing it or switching tabs freezes or ends the room.
- Co-op scores are not ranked.

### Coins and the shop
- Once you are signed in and your details are filled in, every game (solo or co-op) pays coins: more waves, more correct letters and a higher difficulty pay more.
- The first 3 games each day pay in full, later ones 30%. Games shorter than 30 seconds pay nothing.
- Press 商店 · 角色 on the main screen to buy **characters**, **skins**, **scenes** and **weapons**.
  - A character starts with an extra item or a small ability (the medic starts with a medkit, for example).
  - Skins, scenes and weapons only change the look. You need the character before you can buy its skins.
  - Some skins cannot be bought and unlock through achievements (reviving teammates 100 times, for example).
- In co-op your teammates see your character and skin; the scene is the host's.

### Sign-in and leaderboards
- Sign in with Google to put your solo scores on the leaderboards. You can also play without signing in.
- School accounts enter their class and seat number once (for example `J203`, `S1S2`, `S1AC1`). Only a teacher can change it afterwards.
- School accounts see three boards: **my class**, **my grade** and **whole school**. Other Google accounts go on the **public** board.
- Each mode and difficulty has its own board and keeps your best score of the year. Boards start again every year.
